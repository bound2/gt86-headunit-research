the device and entering its existing software version to obtain a matching

files and navigation data. This is a more relevant lead than only trying
latest-update links. However, the guide's historical availability statement
does not verify today's catalogue or this specific release.
[Toyota reconfiguration guide, sections 3.1 and 3.2](https://mapupdatecontent.toyota-europe.com/Documents/Dealer%20Guide/MapReconfiguration_16MM_DealerGuide_English_Split_map_update.pdf).

reconfiguration and map/software updates under **Standard Subscription**, not
free registration. No signed-in catalogue was accessed, account created,

[Toyota TechDoc subscription access table](https://www.toyota-tech.eu/subscription-plans).

The US Toyota firmware portal explicitly excludes navigation/map downloads
and limits its software to US-market vehicles. It is not an appropriate

[Toyota US firmware portal scope](https://www.toyota.com/firmware-updates).
